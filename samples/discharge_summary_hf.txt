Patient: R. Menon, 67-year-old male. Admitted 3 March 2026, discharged 8 March 2026.

Presenting Complaint

Three days of increasing breathlessness, orthopnoea and ankle swelling.

Diagnosis

Precipitant thought to be non-adherence to furosemide and a high-salt diet.
